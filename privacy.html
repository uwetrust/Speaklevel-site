Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to prepare your audit, deliver what you buy, and reply to you.
  • We never sell your information.
  • A few trusted services help us run SpeakLevel (form delivery, bookings, checkout). Some of them store data outside Australia.
  • You can ask to see, fix or delete your information at any time: hello@speaklevel.com.au.

1. Who we are

SpeakLevel provides workplace English coaching and resources for migrants in Australia. SpeakLevel is operated by J M Henry (ABN 55 797 950 739), New South Wales, Australia. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean SpeakLevel.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What we collect

When you fill in the Job-Readiness Audit intake form

  • Your full name and email address
  • How confident you feel using English at work
  • Where you are based, and your city or suburb (optional)
  • Your employment situation, target industry and biggest English challenge at work
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in the notes box

When you book a call

Bookings are made through Calendly. We receive your name, email address, the time you booked and any answers you give in the booking form.

When you buy something

Purchases (such as the Employment Readiness Kit or the Foundations Guide) are processed by Payhip. We receive your name, email address and what you bought. We never see or store your card details — payments are handled by Payhip and its payment providers.

When you email us

We keep your email and our reply so we can help you.

Information we ask you not to send

We don’t need sensitive information such as health details, religion or visa and immigration status to help you. Please don’t include it in the form or in emails.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare for and run your free Job-Readiness Audit
  • To reply to your questions and send you what you asked for
  • To deliver products you buy and provide support
  • To improve our coaching and resources (using information that doesn’t identify you, where possible)
  • To send you emails about SpeakLevel only if you have agreed. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Services that help us run SpeakLevel

We use these services. Each has its own privacy policy, and some store information on servers outside Australia (mainly in the United States):

  • FormSubmit — delivers intake form answers to our inbox
  • Calendly — bookings and calendar invites
  • Payhip — checkout and digital downloads
  • Fontshare — loads the fonts on this website (your browser shares your IP address with it, like any website)
  • Our website host and email provider — host this website and our inbox

We only share what each service needs to do its job.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. Calendly and Payhip may set their own cookies when you use their booking or checkout pages.

6. How we store and protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then delete it.

7. See, correct or delete your information

Email hello@speaklevel.com.au to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to fix anything that’s wrong, or to have it deleted. We’ll respond within 30 days.

8. Questions or complaints

If you have a concern about how we’ve handled your information, please email hello@speaklevel.com.au first and we’ll work with you to fix it. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
