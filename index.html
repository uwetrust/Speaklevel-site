Try It Free

Three Real Workplace Phrases. Try Them Now.

This is the kind of English the Kit teaches — practical, professional, Australian.

Asking for help

Direct: “I don't understand. Tell me again.”

Australian: “Sorry, would you mind running me through that one more time?”

Why it works: softens the request and keeps you professional.

When you're behind

Direct: “I cannot finish today.”

Australian: “Just a heads up — I'm running a bit behind on this. Can I get it to you tomorrow morning?”

Why it works: shows ownership and offers a fix.

Disagreeing politely

Direct: “No, that is wrong.”

Australian: “I see what you mean. Could we also look at it from this angle?”

Why it works: keeps your voice in the room without conflict.
